# Santa Clara public-data limitations

These gaps could not be closed. They come from the free sources, the Assessor's resale rule, gateway refusals, or this Mac's memory. They are not steps this submission skipped. The county archive, the tables, the query table, and the San Jose roof list are published.

## Records the free sources do not contain

- **Owner names.** The public GIS layer `ubcd-cewv` has no owner name, and the county open-data catalog has no parcel owner, mailing, or sale table. The only countywide list of the top taxpayers is ten firm names, not a parcel roll. The Assessor’s free per-parcel sheet omits the assessee name. The office states that this follows Government Code 6254.21, and the sheet’s terms prohibit resale. The paid Secured Master File (MF901B, about $495) is the bulk file that contains owner and billing names. It was not bought. The Clerk-Recorder’s official-record index has not been online since 2018, so grantor and grantee names are not a download. California Secretary of State BizFile does not collect real-property ownership.
- **Ten-year holds and out-of-area owners, as a published table.** The same free sheet does print a transfer date, a mailing address, a document number, and assessed values, one APN at a time, at `https://www.sccassessor.org/apps/showPdfSearchResult.aspx?ApnValue=<8-digit-APN>&SType=rp`. A transfer date answers “held more than 10 years.” A mailing city that differs from the situs answers “out of the area,” without an owner name. Checked live on 2026-10-02, the five San Jose roofs older than 15 years are: `46204039` transferred 2022-03-25 and the mailing city matches San Jose; `49454036` transferred 2016-08-15 and the mailing city matches San Jose; `56904039` transferred 2025-02-19 and the mailing city matches San Jose; `56934012` transferred 2026-03-19 on a revocable transfer-on-death deed and the mailing city matches San Jose; `67620085` transferred 2014-04-02 and the mailing city is Cupertino. Only `49454036` and `67620085` have been held more than 10 years as of 2026-10-02. Only `67620085` has a mailing city other than the situs city, and that city is still inside Santa Clara County. Identifier-proven APN `09201021` has a mailing city of Oakland, which is outside the county, and a transfer date of 2016-11-16. Those sheets were not copied into the county archive. The terms allow research of individual parcels and prohibit resale, and a full pass is about 494,841 separate PDFs. The paid Condensed Sales File is a two-year transfer list, so buying it would still miss the parcels that have not transferred in more than 10 years.
- **Year built.** No free countywide year-built field was loaded. San Jose’s large-building year-built file covers buildings of about 20,000 square feet and up. It is not a house file, and year built is not a roof date.
- **Assessed value in the archive.** Land, improvement, and net assessed values are on the same per-parcel sheet. They are not in the GIS layer and were not loaded into the published tables.
- **Lot area.** `lot_area_sqft` is null. `shape_area` has no proven unit.
- **BBB ratings.** BBB has no free bulk download. The official API needs approval and does not allow public display of complaints. No BBB score was invented.
- **Legal business identity.** California Secretary of State BizFile is behind Incapsula. It was not bypassed. CSLB license rows for a Santa Clara C-39 pilot stay in a private snapshot and are not emitted as lexicon company, person, or license records. The live lexicon has no license class for this emit.
- **Countywide roof age.** Roof age is the San Jose extract only: ArcGIS permit layers 7, 8, and 9, `WORKDESC = 'ReRoof'`, terminal date `FINALDATE` only. Census on 2026-10-01: 7,787 features, 6,955 APNs, 59 accepted completed primary roof replacements, 5 roofs older than 15 years (`46204039`, `49454036`, `56904039`, `56934012`, `67620085`). Issue date is not used as roof age. `builtYear` is null.
- **Long-open roofing permits for the other 15 jurisdictions.** Unincorporated Santa Clara, Campbell, Cupertino, Gilroy, Los Altos, Los Altos Hills, Los Gatos, Milpitas, Monte Sereno, Morgan Hill, Mountain View, Palo Alto, Santa Clara city, Saratoga, and Sunnyvale have no harvester in this run. San Jose is a vendor-module proof and is not registered in the runtime permit feed.
- **Parcels with no APN.** The GIS layer has 504,717 features. 9,876 have no usable APN and are excluded. 494,841 valid APNs were published. GIS is the seed under an approved exception. It is not the paid secured roll (public order-form size about 458,000).

## What a demo should say out loud

Owner names are not in this free dataset. A transfer date and a mailing address exist on the Assessor’s individual property sheet, and those sheets were not republished. Year built, BBB ratings, and countywide roof age are not in this dataset. Roof age older than 15 years is five San Jose parcels, not the county. Open-permit history for the other cities was not harvested.
